# Planned commercial plans

Starter RM299/month: Local AI, 2 employees, 1 PC. Business RM599: Cloud AI, 5 employees, 1 PC. Enterprise RM1,499+: Hybrid AI, 10+ employees, multiple PCs. Prices and credit allowances must become configurable; these are proposals, not enforced entitlements.

Existing supported ERP/POS/WMS/TMS/API connectors must be available to every tier. Custom connector development/maintenance may be billed separately. Credits, BYOK, additional devices, setup fees and subscription expiration are planned. Trusted backend validation is required before real billing; local SQLite must never prove payment.
