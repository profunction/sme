# Connector framework requirements (planned)

No connectors ship in Phase 1. Future connectors must declare identity, authentication, capabilities, read/write permissions, connection tests, retry/rate policies and audit logging. Use official APIs/SDKs first, then authorized databases or controlled browser/Windows automation. Require approval for inventory, price, refund, messaging and deletion writes. Mock ERP/POS/WMS/TMS tests do not establish support for real vendors. Connector categories remain available across all tiers.
